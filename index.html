The Problem Statement

Transitioning to college life involves a major shift in dining habits. New students (FE & DSY) often face hidden costs, hygiene concerns, and the dreaded "Sunday Night Hunger" when most messes close. MessMitra provides transparency through on-ground audited data.

Sustainable Goals

Aligned with UN SDG 3 (Good Health & Well-being) and SDG 11 (Sustainable Cities & Communities).

Field Methodology

14 private messes audited physically on Aug 12 & Aug 19, 2026. Verified pricing, water hygiene, and seating.
